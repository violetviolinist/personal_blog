Like me, AI and its oncoming ramifications must be on your mind since ChatGTP came out. The advent and dominance of AI or any technology for that matter ties intricately with the political and social orders of our world. Two politically polarized states will produce vastly different outcomes from said technology. The US currently happens to be at the forefront of this advancement, just like we have seen before with other technologies. Which means, what we have mostly seen so far is how AI may influence and transform a bona fide capitalist society. The narratives built around it, the promises made, the fears expressed, all of it has occurred under one specific context. In my opinion, the answer to a lot of questions like whether AI will take away your job or not has more to do with policy rather than the technology itself. And policy is not constant. It is made to evolve to accommodate for most problems that crop up in a society. At least in an ideal world. The discussion should then be about how far we stray from the ideal. The simple point I am trying to make is that how AI will affect you is a deeply political question, and must be looked at through that lens.
